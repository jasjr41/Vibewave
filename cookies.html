Cookie Policy

Learn how VIBEWAVE uses cookies and manage your preferences to enhance your music experience.

About Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our service.

Why We Use Cookies

At VIBEWAVE, we use cookies to:

  • Remember your login details and preferences
  • Provide personalized music recommendations
  • Analyze how our service is used to make improvements
  • Ensure the security of your account
  • Deliver relevant content and advertisements

Cookie Settings

You can manage your cookie preferences below. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect your experience on our website.

Essential Cookies

Required for the website to function properly. These cannot be disabled.

Preference Cookies

Remember your settings, volume levels, and equalizer preferences.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how users interact with VIBEWAVE to improve our service.

Marketing Cookies

Used to deliver personalized music recommendations and advertisements.

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Session Cookies Maintain your login session and remember your actions during your visit Until you close the browser
Preference Cookies Remember your settings, theme preferences, and audio settings 1 year
Analytics Cookies Collect information about how you use VIBEWAVE to help us improve 2 years
Marketing Cookies Track your interests to provide relevant music recommendations 6 months

Third-Party Cookies

We may also use cookies from trusted third-party services to enhance your experience:

  • Google Analytics: Helps us understand how users interact with our website
  • Payment Processors: Necessary for processing subscription payments
  • Social Media Platforms: Enable social sharing features
  • Advertising Partners: Help us deliver relevant music-related content

Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies are stored and delete them
  • Block cookies from specific sites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Clear all cookies when you close the browser
