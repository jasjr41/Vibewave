Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create playlists in VIBEWAVE?

To create a playlist, navigate to the "My Playlists" section and click the "+ New Playlist" button. Give your playlist a name and start adding songs from your library. You can drag and drop songs to rearrange them.

Can I download songs for offline listening?

Yes! With a VIBEWAVE Premium subscription, you can download songs for offline listening. Simply click the download icon next to any song or playlist. Downloaded content will be available in your "Offline" section.

How do I adjust audio quality settings?

Go to Settings > Audio Quality to adjust your streaming and download quality. Higher quality uses more data but provides better sound. We recommend High Quality (256kbps) for most listeners.

Why is my music skipping or buffering?

Buffering issues are usually related to your internet connection. Try these solutions:

  • Check your internet connection speed
  • Switch to a lower audio quality in Settings
  • Close other apps that might be using bandwidth
  • Restart the VIBEWAVE application
How do I share my playlists with friends?

To share a playlist, go to your playlist, click the three-dot menu, and select "Share". You can share via direct link, social media, or messaging apps. Your friends will need a VIBEWAVE account to listen to the full playlist.

Getting Started

Setting Up Your Profile

Customize your VIBEWAVE experience by setting up your profile. Add a profile picture, select your favorite genres, and tell us about your musical preferences to get better recommendations.

Exploring Music

Use the Discover section to find new music based on your listening habits. Our algorithm learns what you like and suggests new artists and albums you might enjoy.

Using the Equalizer

Access the equalizer in Settings > Audio to customize your listening experience. Choose from presets or create your own custom settings for different genres or listening environments.

About Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our service.

Why We Use Cookies

At VIBEWAVE, we use cookies to:

  • Remember your login details and preferences
  • Provide personalized music recommendations
  • Analyze how our service is used to make improvements
  • Ensure the security of your account
  • Deliver relevant content and advertisements
